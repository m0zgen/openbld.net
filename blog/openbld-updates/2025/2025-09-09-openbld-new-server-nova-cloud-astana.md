The first DNS server in 🇰🇿 Kazakhstan’s capital has been launched with the support of [NOVA Cloud](https://novacloud.kz/).
{/* truncate */}
OpenBLD is now even closer to its users — both digitally and physically.

Fast response, reliable filtering, phishing and tracker protection — now right next door.

🔧 What’s new with the Astana node launch:

- Even faster response times in the capital and northern regions
- Available over secure protocols: DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) and DNS-over-TLS (DoT)
- Reduced latency and smarter traffic distribution
- DNS filtering is now even more stable under high load
- ✅ Geo-distributed traffic between major cities: Astana, Almaty, Shymkent

Huge thanks to NOVA Cloud Kazakhstan for their trust and technical support!

This is a significant step for Kazakhstan’s digital ecosystem and the development of a safe internet — when the infrastructure is deployed here, in Kazakhstan 🇰🇿

💡 An internet without noise, tracking, or cyber threats — it’s not magic, it’s OpenBLD.net
